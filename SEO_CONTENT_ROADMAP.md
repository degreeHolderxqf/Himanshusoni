## 1. Current Architecture Evaluation vs. Future Strategic Expansion

The website currently contains 7 high-impact, fully pre-rendered static routes:
1. `/` — Home (Primary positioning & conversion hub)
2. `/website-portfolio` — Full case studies repository with interactive filtering & search
3. `/services` — Detailed breakdown of 8 core technical specializations
4. `/approach` — 6-Phase engineering methodology & deliverables
5. `/aboutus` — Background, technical skills matrix & academic credentials
6. `/planner` — Interactive 4-step project scoping wizard
7. `/contactus` — Direct inquiry & consultation booking

### Strategic Assessment: Keep Consolidated vs. Expanding Thin Pages
- **Current Assessment:** Rather than creating 15 thin, near-duplicate subpages that dilute link equity and risk doorway penalties, the existing consolidated `/services` and `/website-portfolio` architecture concentrates domain authority.
- **Next Phase Strategy:** As organic impressions grow in Google Search Console, high-opportunity clusters should be developed into dedicated, original long-form pages only when they provide substantial, unique technical depth.

### 2.1 Specification for Each Dedicated Service Page:
Each proposed service page must follow a strict commercial framework:
1. **Target Business Problem:** What operational bottleneck or revenue limitation does the merchant face?
2. **Who Is It For:** D2C brands outgrowing standard themes, Plus brands needing custom checkout rules, or stores suffering from app-bloat.
3. **What Is Included:** Detailed scope of work (modular Liquid sections, custom Functions logic, API endpoints).
4. **Engineering Process:** Step-by-step implementation from audit to QA.
5. **Demonstrated Proof:** Embedded case studies showing verified outcomes (e.g., *Alpha Tech Apparel* for custom apps; *Speedgear* for migration).
6. **Required Client Inputs:** What information the merchant should provide before requesting a quote.
7. **Direct Conversion CTA:** Link directly to the `/planner` with pre-selected service type.
